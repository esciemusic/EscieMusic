# CASE FILES — PUBLIC DISCOVERY BRIDGE

## Purpose

Case Files are the controlled bridge between ESCIE's private investigation/creation work and the public ESCIE universe.

They are **not** a public puzzle board and they are **not** a mirror of Central's private workspace.

A Case File may contain research, observations, multidisciplinary work, candidate relationships, artifacts, questions, contradictions, and publication history. Only material explicitly approved for public exposure can cross the boundary.

## Three-layer model

```
CENTRAL
Private brain / research / creation / organization
        ↓
CASE FILES
Curated investigation / context / deeper layers
        ↓
PUBLIC ESCIE
Art / places / sensory work / archive / relationships
        ↓
AUDIENCE
Discovery / interpretation / personal meaning
        ↓
CASE FILES
Deeper context may become available over time
```

## Public experience rule

The audience should encounter finished works as discoveries.

Do not default to:
- clue-hunt language
- puzzle instructions
- "solve this" prompts
- artificial evidence boards
- forced connections
- explanations of every symbol
- premature project/track reveals

The desired feeling is:

**"I discovered this."**

not:

**"ESCIE is asking me to solve this."**

## Case File layers

### Layer 1 — Discovery

Minimal public-facing entry. An artifact may exist without explaining its origin.

### Layer 2 — Context

Approved background, source material, places, dates, references, process notes, or related creative material.

### Layer 3 — Investigation

Approved deeper material: evidence, timelines, relationships, competing interpretations, contradictions, and unanswered questions.

### Layer 4 — Restricted

Private Central material that must never become public automatically: raw/unreleased source material, sensitive information, unresolved claims, private notes, and artist-only decisions.

## Artifact pipeline

SOURCE → DISCIPLINES → OBSERVATIONS → ASSOCIATIONS → METAPHOR / ALLEGORY → MEDIA EXPERIMENT → CANDIDATE ARTIFACT → EDITORIAL REVIEW → ARTIST APPROVAL → PUBLIC DISCOVERY → ARCHIVE / CONNECTIONS

Disciplines can include history, architecture, geography, photography, film, forensics, psychology, sociology, mythology, material culture, musicology, literature, art, cartography, mathematics and archival science.

Disciplines generate possibilities. They do not dictate meaning.

| THE WORLD | Montréal places, routes, geography, architecture, maps and environmental observations |

| ESCIE | Artist identity, philosophy, selected context and deliberately unknown material |

## Publication rule

A Case File can exist long before its public layer does.

Public visibility is an explicit state, not an automatic consequence of research being complete.

Artist approval remains the final authority for canonical public release.

## November 22, 2026

The November 22 discovery sequence is the first controlled manifestation of this architecture.

Its artifacts should appear publicly through ART, THE WORLD and GLASS EYE. The Case File remains the deeper source/bridge. BAM and Dallas '63 remain sealed until their approved reveal.

## Design principle

The Case Files should make the universe deeper over time without making the public experience heavier.

The audience should be able to enjoy an artifact without investigating it, and investigate it without being forced to accept a single meaning.
