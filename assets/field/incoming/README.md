# ESCIE FIELD / INCOMING

This folder is the drop point for photographs and visual material captured for the ESCIE World.

## Workflow

1. Drop new photographs into `assets/field/incoming/`.
2. Do not rename or edit them just to make them fit the site.
3. Tell ESCIE: **new photos**.
4. The images can then be reviewed for:
   - subject
   - place
   - moment
   - atmosphere
   - World location
   - related symbols/glyphs
   - visual treatment
   - public visibility
   - possible connections to existing records
5. Approved material can be moved into the appropriate public asset/content location.

## Principle

The photograph is the source.

Treatment is secondary.

The World decides where the image belongs; the image does not have to be forced into a predefined aesthetic.

Do not expose private, unreleased, or sensitive material publicly without explicit approval.

## Naming

Original camera filenames are acceptable for intake. Do not add meaning to a filename that has not been established.

## Status

**INCOMING = captured, not yet classified or published.**
