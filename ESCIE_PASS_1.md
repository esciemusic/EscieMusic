# ESCIE — PASS 1

## Purpose

The final section is the artist's presence, not another information dashboard.

ESCIE is the artist and the public endpoint for identity, philosophy, and selected context. It should feel like a quiet return to the person behind the work.

## Pass 1 direction

- Keep the section sparse.
- Keep **ESCIE IS THE ARTIST. THE WORK SPEAKS FROM HERE.** as the primary public statement.
- Present the artist identity without turning the section into a biography.
- Preserve the principle that the art does not create the universe; the universe creates the art.
- Keep Montréal present as context, without unnecessary status/year metadata.
- Keep social links available as external signal, but do not repeat a large social dashboard.
- Do not reintroduce a reality-classification system.
- Do not expose unreleased BAM/Dallas material.
- Do not turn private Central identity metadata into public canon.
- Keep the section compatible with EN/FR/ES site controls.

## Public shape

**ESCIE**

ESCIE IS THE ARTIST. THE WORK SPEAKS FROM HERE.

The art does not create the universe. The universe creates the art.

External signal / social links may follow.

The section ends there.

## Boundary

Public ESCIE identity is a finished presentation layer. Central may hold richer private biography, research, relationships, drafts, and operational metadata. None of that crosses into the public page automatically.

## Pass 2 readiness

Pass 2 should harden the identity presentation, multilingual behavior, responsive spacing, and interaction/a11y without adding unnecessary content.
