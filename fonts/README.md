# ESCIE Font Family

Three related faces serve three different layers of the ESCIE experience.

## ESCIE
Identity, major titles, and section names. Direction: gothic display, damaged elegance, narrow authority, human rather than fantasy-horror. Reference direction: Angel Wish-derived gothic display language, rebuilt as an original ESCIE face.

## ESCIE SIGNAL
Navigation, metadata, evidence, interface, and operational language. Direction: industrial display, clean geometry, restrained monospace influence, readable on a phone.

## ESCIE SCRIPT
Intimate visitor-facing traces, personal notes, and handwriting moments. Direction: imperfect handwriting, human pressure and rhythm, intimate without becoming decorative. Reference direction: Chopin Script-derived handwriting language, rebuilt as an original ESCIE face.

## Implementation rule
The site uses deliberate fallbacks so typography never breaks while original font binaries are developed. Do not substitute an external web-font service into production. Final binaries belong here only after licensing/provenance and glyph coverage are confirmed.

The three faces are one family: related in proportion, spacing, and contrast while serving different layers of the ESCIE experience.
