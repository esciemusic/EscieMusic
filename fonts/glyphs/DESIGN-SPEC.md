# ESCIE Glyph Design Specification — Pass 1

## Shared construction

The glyph family should feel like one writing system even though its sources differ.

- monochrome first
- one optical stroke family
- geometric skeleton with controlled organic irregularity
- no ornamental frames unless the meaning requires one
- readable at 20–24px
- recognizable at 64–128px
- generous negative space
- no generic emoji language
- no permanent glow
- no filled black blobs as the default
- distortion is an interface state, not part of the base anatomy

The six portal glyphs are not six unrelated logos. They are six verbs in one visual grammar.

## OJÚ — SEE

Source:
Yorùbá concepts of ojú òde (outer eye) and ojú inú (inner eye).

ESCIE construction:
- one clear outer eye
- a second, quieter inner-eye layer
- the inner layer should feel like perception moving inward, not a second eyeball
- avoid a literal cultural glyph claim
- avoid eyelashes, cartoon anatomy, or generic "eye icon" styling

Interaction:
- dormant: outer contour
- active: inner eye resolves
- connected: inner eye can reveal the route/relationship

## MAYA SPEECH — HEAR

Source:
Maya/Mesoamerican speech-scroll convention representing speech, song, breath, sound, and related ephemeral phenomena.

ESCIE construction:
- the mark must visibly originate from a mouth/voice source
- the outgoing form becomes a curling transmission path
- the curl should be asymmetrical and alive
- do not invent a "Maya alphabet" character
- do not use a generic chat bubble

Interaction:
- dormant: compact voice mark
- active: scroll opens
- connected: curl extends toward the next audio relationship

## THOTH — READ

Source:
Ancient Egyptian Thoth and scribal traditions.

ESCIE construction:
- writing action is primary
- use the relationship between writing instrument, surface, and recorded mark
- an ibis reference may inform silhouette but should not become a literal deity portrait
- avoid fake hieroglyphs
- avoid generic modern book iconography

Interaction:
- dormant: compact writing mark
- active: writing stroke appears
- connected: stroke can become a path into text/knowledge

## VITRUVIAN MAN — MEET

Source:
Leonardo da Vinci's Vitruvian Man.

ESCIE construction:
- this is a source-artwork mark, not an invented historical glyph
- use the original public-domain artwork when the artwork itself is shown
- do not trace a simplified human and call it Vitruvian Man
- the circle/square relationship is part of the work and must not be casually redesigned

Interaction:
- dormant: restrained source-artwork mark
- active: artwork resolves
- connected: relationships radiate from the human subject

Production gate:
Source artwork provenance must be documented before binary embedding.

## STARGATE — GO

Source:
ESCIE original interpretation of celestial threshold / passage.

ESCIE construction:
- concentric threshold geometry
- one readable opening
- celestial reference without science-fiction machinery
- the center should feel like a place that can be entered
- avoid the literal Stargate franchise visual language

Interaction:
- dormant: closed threshold
- active: aperture opens
- connected: destination appears through the opening

## ORION — CONNECT

Source:
The actual Orion constellation.

ESCIE construction:
- preserve recognizable Orion star placement
- use actual constellation geometry rather than a generic star network
- the connecting lines are the trace/path
- allow some stars to remain visually quiet
- never turn it into a random web icon

Interaction:
- dormant: sparse stars
- active: constellation resolves
- connected: relevant trace line brightens

## System rule

The symbol should remain meaningful when removed from the interface.

The interface should remain usable when the symbol is removed.

That is the balance:
**language, not dependency.**
