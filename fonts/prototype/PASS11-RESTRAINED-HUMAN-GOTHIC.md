# ESCIE Pass 11 — Restrained Human Gothic

Prototype-only. Production fonts remain unchanged.

Pass 10 was rejected after visual review: its softened geometry became
too irregular and introduced malformed-looking joins, while losing the
controlled Gothic structure.

Pass 11 returns to Pass 9's successful silhouette discipline and makes
smaller corrections:

- preserve tall/narrow Gothic-medieval authority;
- preserve solid, readable silhouettes;
- keep curves smooth where they improve C/G/O/R/S;
- use restrained tapered terminals rather than rounded or techno corners;
- keep E/A/I/T structural and legible;
- keep SIGNAL calmer through the existing clean mode;
- keep SCRIPT separate;
- no texture, glitch, distress, pixel decoration, or fantasy-horror ornament.

## Acceptance gate

1. no malformed joins or missing-looking strokes;
2. immediate ESCIE recognition;
3. Gothic/medieval authority without generic blackletter;
4. less techno/pixel than Pass 9;
5. less irregular than Pass 10;
6. readable at 390px;
7. clean French accents;
8. production fonts remain untouched.
