# ESCIE Letterform Blueprint

## ESCIE
- narrow, heavy display proportions
- architectural verticals
- beveled / interrupted terminals
- controlled damage, never decorative horror
- strong negative-space counters
- authority with a human imperfection
- should carry: Father / Man / Monster

## ESCIE SIGNAL
- related proportions to ESCIE, but cleaner
- operational geometry
- consistent rhythm and spacing
- restrained technical character
- optimized for metadata, navigation and evidence

## ESCIE SCRIPT
- same family proportions beneath the handwriting
- visible human pressure and irregularity
- connected-feel strokes
- intimate rather than romantic
- reserved for visitor-facing personal traces

## Shared family rule
The three faces should look like three states of one visual system, not three unrelated fonts.
