# ESCIE Pass 12 — Filled Curved Silhouettes

Prototype-only. Production fonts remain unchanged.

Pass 11 exposed a structural problem: curved anchors built from thick stroke
bands can self-intersect or create broken-looking raster edges.

Pass 12 changes the construction method, not the identity direction.

- C / G / R / S use one filled outer+inner contour.
- O uses a clean filled ring.
- M uses one non-self-crossing filled silhouette.
- A / E / I / T retain strong structural forms.
- Tall/narrow Gothic-medieval authority remains.
- Terminals stay restrained and slightly tapered.
- No texture, glitch, distress, pixel decoration, or fantasy-horror ornament.

Acceptance:
1. solid contours;
2. no missing-looking strokes;
3. no self-intersections;
4. immediate ESCIE recognition;
5. less techno/pixel than Pass 9;
6. less irregular than Pass 10/11;
7. readable at 390px;
8. clean French accents.
