# ESCIE Font Pass 8 — Authored Letterform Refinement

Status: **PROTOTYPE ONLY**

Pass 8 follows the Pass-7 narrow-Gothic construction. Its purpose is not to make the font stranger; it is to make the letterforms feel deliberately authored rather than mechanically constructed.

## Direction

- Keep the tall, narrow Gothic/medieval authority.
- Keep the monochrome silhouette clean.
- Move asymmetry into terminals, joins, counters, and rhythm.
- Avoid damage, texture, glitch, ornament, and fantasy-horror decoration.
- Preserve French/English readability at phone scale.
- Keep SIGNAL cleaner than ESCIE.
- Keep SCRIPT as a separate handwritten state.

## Anchor refinement

- **A:** slightly unequal shoulders with a lower structural crossbar.
- **C:** unequal architectural terminals.
- **E:** clearly shorter middle arm with a controlled rhythm.
- **G:** proprietary internal bar with a decisive vertical return.
- **I:** authored stem rather than a generic serif I.
- **M:** controlled unequal inner descent.
- **O:** narrow stable counter without distortion.
- **R:** stronger bowl-to-leg transition.
- **S:** related upper/lower masses with controlled join tension.
- **T:** heavy cap and narrow authored stem.

## Acceptance gate

Pass 8 is not production-approved by generation alone.

The specimen must pass:

1. desktop monochrome inspection;
2. 390px phone inspection;
3. ESCIE wordmark recognition;
4. Montréal readability;
5. French accents and navigation labels;
6. distinction between ESCIE / SIGNAL / SCRIPT;
7. no generic stock-Gothic reading;
8. no glitch/distressed reading.

If it fails any of these, production stays on the safe fallback.

## Production boundary

No release font is changed by Pass 8.
