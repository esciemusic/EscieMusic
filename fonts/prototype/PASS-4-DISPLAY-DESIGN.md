# ESCIE Typography Pass 4 — Display Face

Status: prototype only. This branch must not alter production typography.

## Objective

Move the ESCIE display face away from the previous technical/pixel geometry and toward the approved identity direction:

- architectural gothic tension
- narrow authority
- damaged elegance
- interrupted terminals
- controlled damage
- stronger organic rhythm
- human rather than fantasy-horror

The reference language is Angel Wish-derived gothic display direction, but every production glyph must remain original.

## Signature glyph review

Pass 4 gives individual construction priority to:

- A — tall apex, compressed counter, deliberate crossbar
- E — narrow stem with unequal terminal lengths
- G — controlled opening and internal bar
- R — compact bowl with aggressive descending leg
- S — asymmetrical upper/lower tension
- M — architectural central descent
- T — heavy top with narrow stem
- C/O — controlled openings rather than decorative damage

## Family rule

The three faces remain separate states of one system:

- ESCIE = identity
- ESCIE SIGNAL = operation
- ESCIE SCRIPT = intimacy

Pass 4 only advances ESCIE. SIGNAL and SCRIPT remain isolated until the display face establishes the family DNA.

## Anti-generic rules

Do not add:
- random horror spikes
- medieval ornament
- fantasy runes
- excessive cracks
- generic blackletter
- copied reference-font outlines
- decorative effects that compensate for weak letterforms

The identity must come from the letter construction itself.

## Acceptance gate

Before any production typography change:

1. ESCIE identity test
2. three-face family relationship test
3. phone readability
4. French/English glyph coverage
5. provenance/licensing review
6. desktop specimen
7. phone specimen
8. user visual approval

Technical green does not equal creative approval.
