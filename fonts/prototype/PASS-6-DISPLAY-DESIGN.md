# ESCIE Typography Pass 6 — Human Pressure

Status: prototype only. Production typography remains untouched.

## Objective

Pass 6 refines the relationships between letters rather than adding more geometry.

The face must remain narrow, authoritative and Gothic while feeling authored by a person with a distinct hand.

## Primary changes

- remove repeated geometric counter cuts where possible
- introduce stroke relationships instead of decorative wedges
- reduce terminal size and repetition
- vary curve pressure subtly between related glyphs
- preserve asymmetric S behavior
- preserve compact R/G bowls
- keep negative space open enough for phone use
- keep the family original and reference-independent

## Identity words

The specimen gate uses:
- ESCIE
- FATHER
- MAN
- MONSTER
- MONTRÉAL
- ART
- THE WORLD
- THE ARCHIVES
- CASE FILES

## Anti-generic rules

No:
- blackletter imitation
- fantasy-rune construction
- random spikes
- fake cracks or damage
- decorative glitching
- copied reference outlines
- effects compensating for weak letterforms

## Acceptance gate

Pass 6 is not production-approved until:
1. the full word rhythm reads unmistakably ESCIE
2. signature glyphs form one coherent authored family
3. the face remains readable on phone
4. French/English diacritics remain correct
5. original provenance remains intact
6. desktop specimen passes
7. phone specimen passes
8. user visually approves

Technical green is not creative approval.
