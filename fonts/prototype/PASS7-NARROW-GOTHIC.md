# ESCIE Font Pass 7 — Narrow Gothic Identity

Status: **PROTOTYPE ONLY**

Pass 7 is the next construction experiment after the Pass-6 solid-silhouette cleanup.

## Objective

Move the ESCIE identity toward:

- tall, narrow Gothic/medieval authority;
- dark elegance without fantasy-horror decoration;
- strong vertical rhythm;
- restrained asymmetry;
- solid silhouettes;
- distinctive authored anchors;
- readable French and English text at phone scale.

## Changes

- Added a dedicated Pass-7 prototype generator.
- Added an authored I construction so the identity word has a complete anchor treatment.
- Tightened A toward a compressed apex and structural crossbar.
- Deepened the M central descent.
- Narrowed the O counter relationship.
- Tightened R and T proportions.
- Kept production binaries untouched.
- Kept the public hero on the safe fallback until visual specimen approval.

## Acceptance gate

Pass 7 is **not** approved merely because it builds.

It must be judged in monochrome at desktop and 390px phone width.

Reject if it reads as:

- generic stock Gothic;
- techno/pixel;
- glitch/distressed;
- decorative horror;
- unreadable on mobile.

Approve only if the letterforms themselves carry ESCIE identity without effects.

## Production boundary

No release font is changed by this pass.
