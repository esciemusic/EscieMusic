# Temporary typography candidate: Oswald + Space Grotesk

## Status
Active temporary public-site typography while the authored ESCIE family remains in prototype review.

## Roles
- **Oswald** — ESCIE identity, hero, section titles, strong labels.
- **Space Grotesk** — navigation, metadata, interface, body copy.
- **System script fallback** — intimate/visitor handwriting until ESCIE SCRIPT is approved.

## Why this pairing
The combination creates a tall, compressed architectural display voice with a contemporary geometric interface layer. It is closer to the intended Montréal / urban / documentary / Ghetto Goth direction than the previous generic system-font fallback, without pretending that an off-the-shelf font is the final ESCIE typeface.

## Guardrails
- Do not treat these fonts as the final ESCIE identity.
- Do not remove the authored ESCIE / SIGNAL / SCRIPT prototype work.
- Do not add glow, scanlines, texture, distortion, or other effects to manufacture identity.
- Re-evaluate on both phone and desktop.
- Before long-term use, self-host the selected binaries and record license/provenance.

The first candidate is intentionally the current baseline.
